How we check a deal

  1. Find it live. Every deal on this page was read in a retailer listing or a deals tracker that names the store, 24–26 Nov 2026. Nothing here is copied from last year or guessed.
  2. Do the maths. The discount is the sale price against the usual price, and the two must add up — the % on each card is computed from the prices shown.
  3. Call it honestly. A big % off a raised list price is not a deal. Two listings on this page carry a ROUTINE verdict with the evidence written on the card: Amazon raised the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus list from $49.99 to $69.99 and the Kindle Colorsoft list from $249.99 to $289.99 in August 2026, before the sale.
  4. Show the receipt. Each card names where the price was read and when, and links to the product at that store.
